About us

Twelve years of small hands and big ideas

Butterfly Kindergarten started in one sunny room with nine children. We have grown, but the promise has not changed: know every child by name, and let them learn the way children actually learn — by doing.

Our story

It began with a garden and a good idea

In 2014, two early-years teachers rented a corner house on Maple Grove Lane because it had something most nurseries in town did not: a garden big enough to get properly muddy in. They opened with nine children, a piano and a compost heap.

Today Butterfly is home to four classrooms and more than eighty children, but the ingredients are the same. Children spend part of every day outdoors. Teachers stay with their group for the whole year. And the compost heap is still there — now tended by the Explorers, who are very serious about worms.

We are a licensed early years provider, inspected annually, and proud that many of our families come to us because a neighbour sent them.

Good to know

Questions parents ask us

What are your hours, and do you offer half days?

We are open Monday to Friday, 8:00am to 5:30pm. Half-day places run 8:00am to 1:00pm and include lunch. Both options are available for every age group, subject to space.

How do you handle allergies and special diets?

All meals are cooked on site and we run a nut-free kitchen. Allergies and dietary needs are recorded at enrolment, displayed in the kitchen and reviewed with you each term.

How does settling in work?

New children come for two short visits with a parent before their first full day, then build up over the first week. Your child's lead teacher stays in touch daily until everyone feels at home.

Is there a waiting list?

Usually, yes — especially for September starts. We recommend registering interest six to nine months ahead. Siblings of current children are offered places first.

How do you keep the children safe?

Secure keypad entry, CCTV at all external doors, a signed daily pick-up list and staff background checks renewed every three years. Fire drills run monthly.

Still have questions?

The quickest way to know whether Butterfly is right for your family is to stand in the room while it is full of children. Come and visit.
