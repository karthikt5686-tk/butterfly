Our programmes

A classroom for every stage

Small groups, familiar faces and a rhythm children can count on — from first steps away from home to the year before big school.

🧸
Ages 2 – 3

Caterpillars

Gentle settling-in, sensory play and lots of cuddles as toddlers learn to share a room with new friends.

🎨
Ages 3 – 4

Chrysalis

Messy art, music mornings and the first taste of letters, numbers and stories told out loud.

🔭
Ages 4 – 5

Explorers

Garden science, building projects and small group work that grows confidence and curiosity together.

🎒
Ages 5 – 6

Butterflies

Our school-readiness year: early reading, writing, numeracy and the independence to walk in on day one smiling.

Why families choose us

Learning that feels like playing

Children remember how a place made them feel. That is why our days are built around play, movement and conversation rather than desks and drills — with teachers who notice every child, every day.

  • Qualified, long-staying teachers. Every lead teacher is early-years certified and first-aid trained.
  • A real garden. Raised beds, a mud kitchen and a shaded play yard used rain or shine.
  • Safe by design. Secure entry, CCTV at all doors and a daily signed pick-up list.
  • Fresh, allergy-aware meals. Cooked on site with a rotating four-week menu.
  • Parents in the loop. Photos, notes and milestones shared through our daily app.
More about our approach

A day at Butterfly

  1. 8:00 – 9:00

    Arrival, free play and a warm welcome at the door.

  2. 9:00 – 9:30

    Morning circle: songs, weather chart and the day's plan.

  3. 9:30 – 11:00

    Learning corners — art, blocks, books and early numeracy.

  4. 11:00 – 12:00

    Garden time and outdoor games.

  5. 12:00 – 1:00

    Hot lunch together, then quiet rest for the little ones.

  6. 1:00 – 3:00

    Projects, music, story time and show-and-tell.

  7. 3:00 – 5:30

    Snack, free play and staggered pick-up.
